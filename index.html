~ About the project ~

Reorganising the ground floor, room by room

This 6.0 × 4.5m extension opens the back of the house into one bright, connected space. The old kitchen becomes a pantry and utility room, while the new footprint holds an open-plan kitchen, family and dining area — lit by rooflights over the island and a full run of bifold doors onto the garden, with a fixed picture window along the side wall.

Designed from a RICS measured survey, refined through a run of floor-plan iterations before settling on the current layout.
